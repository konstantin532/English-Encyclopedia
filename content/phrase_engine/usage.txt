# ЧЕРНОВЫЕ правила употребления (status: draft, автор: Claude, 2026-10-27).
# Это не грамматика, а сочетаемость и прагматика: какие слова естественны в конкретной конструкции.
# Формат: имя_набора | слова через пробел | зачем

habitual | drink eat cook bake read write watch play clean wash call text visit study teach speak learn buy make check use wear feed help order drive ride take listen pay paint draw sing open close start finish answer | Present Simple «привычка»: Mia drinks coffee every morning. Глаголы одноразовых действий (sell the phone, choose the sweater, describe the shop) в привычке звучат неестественно.

baby_ok | eat drink sleep laugh smile sneeze play | Что естественно делает младенец; остальные действия с подлежащим baby не генерируются.

rule_verbs | wear wash clean close open pay sign wait call check bring use smoke take finish start listen repeat spell write read turn | must / have to: правила и обязанности (You must wear a uniform). *The baristas must sell some shoes.

polite_verbs | open close help call repeat spell explain bring wait fix check send print sign carry hold wash clean read answer move | Could you …, please?

temporary_adj | afraid alone busy dizzy glad happy hungry lucky nervous proud ready sick sleepy sore sure thirsty tired well excited surprised wet dirty hot cold warm cool empty full noisy quiet cloudy foggy freezing rainy sunny windy | was/were + прилагательное с «вчера»: I was tired yesterday. Постоянные свойства (*The colleges were large two days ago) не генерируются.

temporary_times | yesterday | для was/were и глаголов состояния — только «недавнее прошлое»: yesterday, yesterday morning, last night, last weekend, last + день недели

habitual_buy | #food #drink | buy в привычке — только то, что покупают регулярно: She buys bread every day. *Your family buys a bike every evening.

progressive_ok | wait sign pack carry hold | Present Continuous «прямо сейчас»: кроме habitual — эти действия естественны в моменте.

this_adj | big small large little huge tiny long short tall black blue brown gray green orange pink purple red white yellow new old nice favorite beautiful pretty cheap expensive | This is my favorite song. *That is your happy ant.
