# English Encyclopedia

## Product
An illustrated course and reference atlas of American English for Russian-speaking learners, from the first words onward. Platform: web, rendered by Django. The canonical visual direction is DESIGN.md.

## People and jobs
- An independent learner chooses a lesson, understands an explanation, listens, practices and returns to difficult material.
- A learner studying with a teacher sees assigned lessons and feedback, alongside the independent route.
- A teacher assigns existing lessons to their groups, reviews answers and identifies work needing attention.
- An editor maintains original course content through Django administration.

## Design context
The author explicitly requested the whole site, including every section, card and detail, and delegated the aesthetic choice. Preserve the warm illustrated atlas: Literata, Golos Text, Gentium Plus, paper, forest green, restrained ochre and the existing original illustrations. Reading and the next learning action take priority over decorative chrome. Desktop, tablet and phone are first-class layouts.

## Truth and boundaries
Progress is the real stored result. Self-reported completion is labelled as such; neither a viewed lesson nor an automated exercise certifies mastery or CEFR. Access rules, original course text, account data and teacher/group boundaries are unchanged by visual work. Main is merged only by the author.

## Success
A learner finds the next step quickly, reads long lessons comfortably and uses all controls by keyboard or touch. Every page family shares the same navigation, type, fields, focus and feedback states. Teacher tables remain readable on narrow screens. Visual acceptance includes real browser checks with reduced motion and no horizontal page overflow.
